# binSEC v2
**Status:** Active
**Version:** v2 (Minecraft 26.1.2)
**License:** Community

A community modpack for cybersecurity and computer science students, built for the
Cyber Jedis and our community Minecraft server. 115+ mods on Fabric, tuned for
programming, automation, exploration, combat and performance, with proximity voice
chat and a live BlueMap of the world.

- Programming-first: CC: Tweaked computers and turtles for Lua automation
- 115+ mods including Create, Terralith, Biomes O' Plenty, TACZ and Farmer's Delight
- Iris + Sodium for shaders and a large FPS boost; Distant Horizons for long views
- Simple Voice Chat and Discord Rich Presence for the community server
- Import the ZIP in Prism Launcher, then join 15.235.110.217:25589
